Why Baba?

"Baba" is what many Balkan kids call their grandmother. Mine never wasted a thing: half an onion became tomorrow's soup, stale bread became dessert. I trained in New York and Geneva and cooked in some of New York's best kitchens, but her table is still the one I cook for.
